# Marketing Studio: team guide

Make posts, carousels and short videos of our app just by chatting with Claude. Claude designs everything; Marketing
Studio lets it take screenshots and screen recordings of the app (also of pages behind the login) and turns its
designs into finished image and video files.

## Set up (once)

1. In the **Claude desktop app**, open **Settings → Extensions**, find **Marketing Studio** (added by the owner) and
   click **Install**. (Or, if you were sent the file, double-click `marketing-studio-<version>.mcpb`.)
2. In its settings:
   - **Where to save your work**: leave it as Documents.
   - **Demo account code**: type the code the owner gives you.
3. Start a **new chat** and type: *is the marketing studio ready?* The first time it takes a minute.

Use it in a normal chat (not Code).

## Make something

Just say what you want, for example:

- *Make a 5-slide Instagram carousel on why to use our rental price calculator.*
- *Make a 20-second Reel showing how to filter outfits by city, with this voiceover.*
- *A single post announcing our new rent-vs-buy calculator, square size.*

Claude will:
1. ask a few questions and suggest the idea;
2. look at the app pages and show you quick screenshots;
3. design it and show you the result;
4. change anything you ask ("shorter headline on slide 2", "use the green outfit instead");
5. save the final files and open the folder.

## Showing Claude a page

Name it in plain words (*the mehndi booking tracker*, *my vendor analytics*) and Claude finds it on the site by
itself, new pages included. Or copy the link from your browser's address bar and paste it into the chat.

## A voiceover or music

Chat can't take audio files, so when Claude asks, it opens an **Inbox** folder. Drop your file there (MP3, M4A or
WAV), paste the script into the chat, and say "done". Claude times the video to your voice.

## Where are my files?

In **Documents → Marketing Studio → (your project name)**:
- `exports` has the finished images and videos, ready to post or send on WhatsApp;
- `captures` and `clips` have the screenshots and recordings.

## Good to know

- Videos take a minute or two. Claude says "still working" and carries on by itself.
- It never changes anything on the site or app: it only looks, scrolls and fills in things like the calculator.
- Pages behind the login show the demo account, never real customers.
- Something odd? Tell Claude *it's not working*, or message the owner.
