# Marketing Studio: team guide

Make posts, carousels and short videos of our app just by chatting with Claude. Claude designs everything; Marketing
Studio lets it take screenshots and screen recordings of the app (also of pages behind the login) and turns its
designs into finished image and video files.

## Set up (once)

1. In the **Claude desktop app**, open **Settings → Extensions**, find **Marketing Studio** (added by the owner) and
   click **Install**. (Or, if you were sent the file, double-click `marketing-studio-<version>.mcpb`.)
2. In its settings:
   - **Where to save your work**: leave it as Documents.
   - **Demo account code**: type the code the owner gives you.
3. Start a **new chat** and type: *is the marketing studio ready?* The first time it takes a minute.

Use it in a normal chat (not Code).

## Make something

Just say what you want, for example:

- *Make a 5-slide Instagram carousel on why to use our rental price calculator.*
- *Make a 20-second Reel showing how to filter outfits by city, with this voiceover.*
- *A single post announcing our new rent-vs-buy calculator, square size.*

Claude will:
1. ask a few questions and suggest the idea, then show you a short **storyboard** (each scene: what's on screen,
   the real taps, the words). It waits for your yes before the long work starts, and asks whenever it's unsure;
2. look at the app pages and show you quick screenshots (for videos it records the real taps, in order);
3. design it, check it privately (text sizes, empty space, screens big enough to read) and show you the result
   **right in the chat**, once: swipe through the slides, or play the video;
4. change anything you ask ("shorter headline on slide 2", "use the green outfit instead"). Looking at a slide and
   saying "make this one shorter" works too: Claude knows which slide you're on.

The first time a result shows, Claude asks to display it: click **Always allow**.

## Using a finished piece

Under each result in the chat:
- **Copy slide** / **Copy video**: puts it on your clipboard. Paste it straight into WhatsApp, Canva or an email.
- **Copy all**: puts every slide on the clipboard as files, in order (paste them into WhatsApp, an email or a folder).
- **Copy caption**: the post text Claude wrote for it, ready to paste under the post.
- **HD version** (videos): the first video is a quick half-size preview. When it looks right, press this and the
  full-quality video replaces it in the same place.
- **Show in folder**: opens the folder with the files (they're already saved on your laptop).

While a video is being made, press **Play Snake while you wait**: arrow keys or WASD (or swipe), P to pause, Space or a
tap to play again. It stops by itself the moment your video is ready.

Want an earlier piece back? Ask *show me the calculator carousel again*.

## Showing Claude a page

Name it in plain words (*the mehndi booking tracker*, *my vendor analytics*) and Claude finds it on the site by
itself, new pages included. Or copy the link from your browser's address bar and paste it into the chat.

## A voiceover or music

- **Music made for your video:** just ask, e.g. *calm modern background music*, *warm Indian music* or *modern with
  a little tabla*. Claude writes it to fit the video (soft keys, pads and bass, or tabla, santoor, sitar, tanpura,
  strings…) and you hear it in the preview. It's kept soft and in the background unless you ask for something
  livelier. Ask for changes like any other ("softer", "more tabla", "no music at the end").
- **Your own voiceover or song:** chat can't take audio files, so when Claude asks, it opens an **Inbox** folder.
  Drop your file there (MP3, M4A or WAV), paste the script into the chat, and say "done". Claude times the video to
  your voice.
- If you ask for neither, the video has no sound.

## Where are my files?

Everything is also saved in **Documents → Marketing Studio → (your project name)**:
- `exports` has the finished images and videos;
- `captures` and `clips` have the screenshots and recordings.

## Good to know

- Videos take a minute or two. Claude says "still working" and carries on by itself.
- If it seems stuck for several minutes, type *reset Marketing Studio*: it stops what's running and starts fresh,
  without losing anything saved.
- It needs free memory. Before a video, close what you don't need (extra browser tabs, games, other Claude
  windows), and use a normal chat rather than Cowork, which needs a lot of memory of its own. Claude will say when
  the laptop is low on memory.
- It never changes anything on the site or app: it only looks, scrolls and fills in things like the calculator.
- Pages behind the login show the demo account, never real customers.
- Something odd? Tell Claude *it's not working*, or message the owner.
