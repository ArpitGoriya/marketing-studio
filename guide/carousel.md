# Carousels and posts

Carousels get saved and shared more than any other post when each swipe pays off. Aim for that.

## Size and length

- **1080x1350 (4:5)** for the feed: it takes the most screen. Square (1080x1080) only when asked; 1080x1920 for
  Stories (then follow the 9:16 safe zones in reel.md).
- The profile grid shows a 3:4 crop of a 4:5 post (about 34 px off each side): keep nothing vital at the far edges.
- **5-8 slides** for most ideas (Instagram allows up to 20). Fewer, stronger slides beat filler.

## The structure

1. **Hook** (slide 1): a specific promise or question in 8 words or fewer, set big (80-110 px), plus the most
   telling visual (the result, the screen that matters). A small swipe cue (an arrow or "Swipe") bottom right.
2. **Why it matters** (slide 2): the problem in the viewer's own words.
3. **One point per slide** (slides 3 to n-1): show it with the real screen, the exact thing highlighted. Number the
   points if it's a list ("2/5").
4. **Payoff and action** (last slide): what they get, and one clear action: "Save this for your next booking",
   "Send this to a mehndi artist friend", "Try it free on Anokhi Reet". The wordmark small.

## Type and layout (at 1080 wide)

- Headlines 64-96 px bold, body 34-44 px, labels 26-30 px, never under 26 px. Normal letter spacing.
- About 30 words a slide at most: headline up to 8-10 words, body up to 20.
- Set the line breaks yourself (`<br>`) so lines are balanced; never leave one word alone on the last line. The
  check flags headlines that wrap into more lines than written.
- Margins 64-96 px, the same on every slide; the headline in the same place on every slide; one grid.
- Dark text on cream, or cream/gold on maroon. Never text straight over a busy screenshot: give it a panel.
- Continuity makes people swipe: let an element (a line, a card, the phone) run off the right edge and continue on
  the next slide.

## Screens in slides

- The screen (or the cropped card from it) takes at least 55-60% of the slide height, or is cropped to the part
  that matters and shown at 2x or more. See screens.md.
- Highlight one thing per screen: a soft gold ring or glow, never a cartoon arrow.

## Before you export

check, then look at every slide's picture: is slide 1 a reason to swipe? Can every word be read at phone size? Any
empty band, cut-off text or lonely word? Fix, check again, then export once with the caption.
