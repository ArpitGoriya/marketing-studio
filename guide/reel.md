# Reels, Stories and Shorts (9:16 video)

## Before anything: the storyboard

Agree it with the person before capturing (see the overview): each scene's seconds, the screen, the real action, the
words on screen; the length; music or not. Ask when unsure. Then follow it.

## Size and length

- **1080x1920**, 30 fps for the final (60 only for fast scrolling screens).
- **7-15 s** for one punchy idea, **15-30 s** for showing how something works. Rarely longer.

## Show the real flow

People want to see how easy it is, so show it the way they'll do it:

- **Record the real action** (record tool) in the order the user does it: the starting screen, the tap, the next
  screen, typing the value, the result appearing. One continuous recording, or two or three, beats a series of still
  crops. Taps are filmed as a soft fingertip, so viewers see what was pressed.
- **Play it at a natural speed.** Cut only to skip waiting (loading, long scrolls).
- **The phone stays the star**: big and whole in the frame, the recording playing inside it, with one short line of
  text above or below saying what's happening ("Add a booking", "Payment reminder in one tap").
- **Match the person's need**: if they want the whole journey, show the whole journey; if they want one feature, show
  only that, start to finish.

## Zoom rarely

- The normal shot is the whole phone, big: about 1.5x its real size (590x1280 px), filling the space between the
  safe zones. Most scenes need no zoom at all.
- Zoom in at most once or twice in a video, only where people must read one small detail (the result number, the
  message). Zoom gently (to about 1.4x the phone shot), hold 1.5-2 s, then go back to the whole phone.
- Never zoom on every scene, never keep the camera moving, and never cut the screen into many close-up pieces.

## The first two seconds

- The hook is on screen in the very first frame: a short line (3-7 words) and the most telling visual, usually the
  result. No logo intro, no fade from black.

## Pace and motion

- Calm and clear: one change every 2-3 s is fine; let actions play out.
- Moves are eased (`cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)` or similar), 0.6-0.9 s, then a hold. Text appears with a simple fade
  or a short slide.
- No spinning, no 3D, no swish sounds, no effects for their own sake.

## Text on screen (most people watch muted)

- Every key message is on screen as text: 3-7 words a line, one or two lines at a time, 48-72 px bold (never under 40).
- Cream/white on maroon or dark on cream, outside the phone, never over the screen's own text.

## Safe zones

The app's own buttons and caption cover parts of the frame. Keep text and key screen details out of the top 270 px,
the bottom 350-420 px, and the right 150 px in the lower half. The middle 1080x1350 is the safest area, and it's also
what the profile grid shows as the cover. The check flags text in these zones.

## A shape that works

1. Hook (0-2 s): the result and the promise.
2. The real flow (most of the video): the recorded action in order, a short line of text per step.
3. The result, held long enough to read (zoom in here if it's small).
4. End card (about 2 s): one action and the wordmark (and store badges when inviting downloads).

## Timing to a voiceover

inbox info gives the spoken parts: start each step of the flow on its line, put the words on screen as they're said,
and keep music simple and quiet under speech.

## Before you export

check with the moments you care about in `at` (the hook, each step, the result, the end card). Compare with the
storyboard. Then export the preview once; the person presses "HD version" in the viewer when they're happy, or asks
you for changes.
