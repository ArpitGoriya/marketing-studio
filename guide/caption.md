# The post caption

Pass it with the export as `caption`: it shows under the result with a Copy caption button, ready to paste.

- **The first line is the hook.** It's all people see before "more" (about 125 characters): a promise, a question or
  a relatable moment. Not a greeting, not the brand name.
- **2-4 short lines of value**: what it is, why it helps, in the viewer's words. Line breaks between them.
- **One call to action**: save it, send it to someone, comment a word, try it ("link in bio").
- **3-5 relevant hashtags** at the end (the niche, the city, the brand), not a wall of them.
- The same voice and rules as the design: the brand's tone, none of the claims it forbids (no prices, numbers or
  superlatives in our own words).
- Hindi or Hinglish when the person asks for it or the audience speaks it.

Example:

```
Still tracking mehndi bookings in a diary? 📒

Advance, balance, who's pending, what you spent: all in one place.
Free in the Anokhi Reet app.

Save this for your next wedding season ✨

#mehndiartist #mehndibusiness #bridalmehndi #anokhireet
```
